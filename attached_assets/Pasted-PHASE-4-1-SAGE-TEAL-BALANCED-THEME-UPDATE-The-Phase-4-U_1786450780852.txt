1. APPROVED DESIGN DIRECTION
==================================================

Theme name:

SAGE–TEAL BALANCED

Design character:

Calm
Modern
Fresh
Focused
Light
Professional
Cue-sports oriented

The application should move away from the current dark charcoal-green appearance and become predominantly light.

The visual hierarchy should use:

• warm/light neutral backgrounds
• white cards
• teal as the main application colour
• darker teal for stronger emphasis
• muted sage for secondary information
• slate blue as a complementary accent
• restrained gold for priority / important coaching information
• dark green-charcoal text rather than pure black
• green for success
• muted red/rust for losses/errors

NO pink.
NO magenta.
NO purple-pink shades.
NO neon colours.
